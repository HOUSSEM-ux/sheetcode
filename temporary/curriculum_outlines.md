# Tunisian Baccalaureate Curriculum Outlines

Based on the provided sources, the curriculum focuses on several key domains for the Math, Sciences Expérimentales, and Techniques sections.

## Mathematics
- **Complex Numbers (Nombres Complexes):** Algebraic, trigonometric, and exponential forms. Solving equations in C. Geometric applications (rotations, translations, scaling).
- **Analysis:** Limits, continuity, derivability, study of functions. Primitives and integrals. Logarithmic and exponential functions. Sequences (Suites réelles).
- **Geometry:** Space geometry (lines, planes, spheres). Vectors, dot products, and cross products.
- **Probability:** Conditional probability, independent events, random variables, binomial distribution.
- **Arithmetic (for Math section):** Divisibility, prime numbers, congruences, Bezout's identity, Gauss's theorem.

## Physical Sciences
- **Physics:** 
  - Electricity: RC, RL, and RLC circuits (forced and free oscillations).
  - Mechanics: Kinematics, dynamics (Newton's laws), work and energy, harmonic oscillators, pendulum.
  - Waves: Mechanical waves, sound waves, electromagnetic waves.
  - Nuclear Physics: Radioactivity, nuclear reactions, mass-energy equivalence.
- **Chemistry:**
  - Chemical kinetics (speed of reaction, factors).
  - Chemical equilibrium (Esterification, Hydrolysis).
  - Acids and bases (pH calculations, titration).
  - Redox reactions and electrolysis.

## Option Italien (Italian as a secondary language)
Modules covered:
1. Città e campagna (City and countryside)
2. Moda e made in Italy (Fashion and made in Italy)
3. Sport e benessere (Sport and wellness)
4. Cibo e alimentazione (Food and nutrition)
5. Le Feste Italiane (Italian holidays)
