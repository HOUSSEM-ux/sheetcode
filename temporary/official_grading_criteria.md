# Official Grading Criteria (Le Barème) - Tunisian Baccalaureate

The official grading criteria (le barème) implemented by the Ministry of Education outlines strict guidelines for the examiners during the correction process.

## General Principles
1. **Partial Credit:** Points are awarded for correct methodology even if the final numerical result is incorrect, provided the error is purely computational and does not simplify the problem.
2. **Double Jeopardy (La cascade des erreurs):** If an incorrect result from question (a) is correctly used in question (b), the student should not be penalized again in question (b) unless the incorrect result leads to an absurdity or significantly simplifies the subsequent question.
3. **Alternative Methods:** Any mathematically or scientifically sound alternative method not listed in the official correction guide must be accepted and awarded full points.
4. **Formatting and Clarity:** A small fraction of the grade (often 0.25 to 0.5 points out of 20) may be allocated to the clarity of the presentation, cleanliness of the copy, and proper use of language, although this is rarely formally penalized unless illegible.

## Specific Subject Rules

### Mathematics
- **Justifications:** A result given without the required justification (e.g., limit theorems, continuity before intermediate value theorem) receives zero or minimal points for that specific step.
- **Graphs:** When drawing curves, points are specifically allocated to:
  - The correct placement of asymptotes and tangents.
  - The general shape of the curve reflecting the variations table.
  - Specific intersection points with the axes.

### Physics & Chemistry
- **Units:** Missing or incorrect units usually incur a penalty of 0.25 points per instance, often capped at a maximum deduction for the whole exam.
- **Literal Expressions:** Students are expected to provide the literal expression (formula) before plugging in the numbers. Skipping the literal expression and only providing the numerical calculation can lead to lost points.
- **Significant Figures:** The final result must respect the correct number of significant figures based on the given data.

### Languages (e.g., Italian Option)
- **Reading Comprehension:** Answers must be formulated in complete sentences using the student's own words where possible, rather than copying entire paragraphs from the text.
- **Writing (Production Écrite):** Graded on:
  - Relevance to the topic (Adéquation au sujet).
  - Coherence and cohesion of ideas.
  - Lexical richness and grammatical accuracy.
